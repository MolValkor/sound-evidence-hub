## Label: exploratory

Educational companion experiment. Not powered, not blinded, not medical treatment of insomnia.

## Why we ran it

Jespersen 2022 (Cochrane) found moderate-certainty improvement in **subjective** sleep quality with music listening, but not clear gains on insomnia severity or objective measures. We wanted a tiny lived illustration of diary-only outcomes.

## Method sketch

- n=8 adults with self-described “sometimes hard to wind down” (not diagnosed insomnia required)
- Week 1: usual routine; Week 2: same playlist 30 min before lights-out
- Morning diary: sleep quality 1–5, estimated sleep latency

## Descriptive pattern

Several participants rated Week 2 sleep quality a half-point higher on average. Latency notes were mixed. Without actigraphy or a control week order randomization that we fully counterbalanced, this is anecdote with a spreadsheet.

## Honest line

Matches the literature’s caution: **feeling** better about sleep ≠ curing insomnia.
