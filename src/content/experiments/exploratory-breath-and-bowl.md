## Label: exploratory

This is **not** a randomized trial, **not** medical advice, and **not** evidence that bowls treat anxiety or any disease.

## Protocol sketch

- Adults (n=10 convenience) completed two 8-minute quiet sits on separate days
- Condition A: paced breathing (4–6 breaths/min) with a timer
- Condition B: same breathing cue while a facilitator struck a Tibetan bowl every ~90 seconds
- Outcome: 0–10 tension and calm VAS before/after; free-text notes

## What we saw (descriptive only)

Both conditions tended to lower tension scores. Condition B notes more often mentioned “focus on the decay of the tone.” Differences were small and **uncontrolled** — expectancy and novelty are obvious confounds.

## Takeaway for Sound Evidence

Useful as a **teaching demo** of why pre–post sound-bath stories feel compelling and why they still need controls. See also Goldsby 2017 (no control) and Cai 2025 (high RoB RCTs).
