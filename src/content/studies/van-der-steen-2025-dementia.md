## What was reviewed

Updated Cochrane review of music-based therapeutic interventions for people with dementia. Evidence current to 30 November 2023. Mix of music therapy and other music-based interventions; most settings were nursing homes; at least five sessions.

## Key numbers

- **30 RCTs**, **1,720** randomized; **1,366** in meta-analysis
- Depressive symptoms vs usual care: SMD **−0.23** (moderate-certainty; 9 studies, 441)
- Overall behavioral problems: possible improvement (low-certainty)
- Agitation/aggression: little or no effect (moderate-certainty)
- Cognition, anxiety, well-being: no clear demonstrated benefit vs usual care
- **Long-term (≥4 weeks after end):** no demonstrated lasting effects

## Honest framing

A probably real but **slight** short-term mood benefit in care-home settings — not a cure, not lasting “healing,” and not a cognition drug alternative.
