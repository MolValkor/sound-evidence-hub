## What was reviewed

Aalbers and colleagues synthesized trials of music therapy (credentialed delivery within a therapeutic relationship) for people with depression. Most comparisons were music therapy **plus** treatment as usual versus usual care alone.

## Key numbers (from Cochrane PLAIN / abstract)

- **9 studies**, **421** participants (adolescents to older adults)
- Clinician-rated depression: SMD **−0.98** (moderate-quality evidence; n=219)
- Patient-reported depression: SMD **−0.85** (n=142)
- Secondary signals for anxiety and functioning vs TAU
- Quality of life: uncertain
- vs psychological therapy: evidence uncertain / underpowered

## How to read this on Sound Evidence

Licensed music therapy is not the same as a sound bath or a YouTube frequency track. This review supports a **short-term adjunct** framing for depressive symptoms — not cure language, and not a substitute for standard care.
