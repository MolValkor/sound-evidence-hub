## Design (from published abstract)

Randomized crossover, **n = 51**. Twelve minutes of Himalayan singing bowl versus silence, followed by 20 minutes of directed relaxation.

## What the abstract reports

Greater decline in systolic blood pressure (p = .044) and heart rate (p = .003) with bowl versus silence; diastolic trend p = .073. PANAS fell under both conditions. A hypertensive subgroup (n = 20) showed similar BP changes with bowl **and** silence versus normotensives.

**Abstract-only** paraphrase from Magos handoff — full text not obtained. Cite as a small, exploratory autonomic signal nested inside relaxation, not evidence that bowls treat hypertension or cardiovascular disease. Evidence strength: **weak**.
