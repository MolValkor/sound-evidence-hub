## What was reviewed

Systematic review of clinical studies on singing bowls (PROSPERO CRD42025639808). Nineteen studies spanning 2008–2024.

## Key findings

- Design mix: 9 RCTs, 2 crossovers, 1 non-RCT, 7 case series
- **All assessed RCTs: high risk of bias** (outcome measurement especially; blinding nearly impossible)
- Heterogeneity blocked most meta-analyses (e.g. depression I²=99%, anxiety I²=86%)
- Authors: potential for anxiety/depression/sleep/cognition signals — **not confirmatory**

## Honest framing

Singing-bowl evidence is **weak**. Sounds and rituals can be meaningful wellness practices; that is not the same as clinical proof of disease treatment.
