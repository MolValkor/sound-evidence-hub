## Design

One ~60-minute group sound meditation; convenience sample of 62 adults; mood scales before and after. **No control group.**

## What changed (within-subject)

Large pre–post drops in tension, anger, fatigue, depressed mood, and anxiety; spiritual well-being rose; vigor decreased.

## Honest framing

People often feel better after a guided group session — this study cannot separate bowls from rest, expectancy, group setting, or time. Cite as exploratory mood change after a session, not as proof bowls treat mental illness.
