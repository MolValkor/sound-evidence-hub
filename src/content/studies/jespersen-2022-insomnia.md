## What was reviewed

Cochrane review of music interventions for insomnia in adults. Typical protocol: prerecorded music listening, often 25–60 minutes daily. Performance bias is inherent — participants cannot be blinded to music.

## Key numbers

- **13 RCTs**, **1,007** adults
- Subjective sleep quality (PSQI): MD **−2.79** (moderate-certainty; 10 studies, 708)
- Insomnia severity scales: no clear effect
- Objective sleep (3 studies, 136 people): did not improve
- No adverse events reported in included studies

## Honest framing

Better evidence for **feeling** like sleep improved than for fixing insomnia as a disorder or changing polysomnography. Adjunct wellness language fits; “cures insomnia” does not.
