## What was reviewed

Open-access meta-analysis of music-based interventions for people living with chronic pain. Nine RCTs, 787 patients.

## Key numbers

- Pain: SMD **−0.51** (95% CI −0.72 to −0.30; I²=62%)
- Depression (4 RCTs): SMD **−0.83** (high heterogeneity)
- Anxiety (2 RCTs): not statistically significant
- Quality of life (2 RCTs): not statistically significant

## Limitations noted by authors

Language limits (English/Chinese), few long-term follow-ups, heterogeneous “standard care,” differing VAS ranges.

## Honest framing

One of the better-supported music claims for **perceived pain** as an adjunct. Do not market as replacing analgesics or curing pain conditions.
