## What was scoped

Vibroacoustic therapy (VAT) for adults with pain: low-frequency sound vibration, with or without music. Twenty studies from 430 records; sample sizes often tiny (1–56).

## Authors’ conclusion (paraphrase)

Research is **too sparse** to identify which properties of VAT help pain management. RCTs are needed.

## Honest framing

Interesting device-based research line — not interchangeable with sound baths, and not ready for strong clinical claims.
