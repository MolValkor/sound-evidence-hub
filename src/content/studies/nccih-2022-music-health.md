## Why this source matters

NCCIH’s fact sheet is a careful public summary of music-and-health research. It separates **music therapy** (credentialed profession, therapeutic relationship) from **music medicine** (recorded music delivered by nurses or others).

## Topics covered

Anxiety in medical settings, pain, sleep, dementia, stress, and pointers to the NIH Sound Health initiative with the Kennedy Center.

## How we use it

As an institutional **overview** card on Sound Evidence — a place to send readers who want NIH-framed language rather than a single paper’s abstract.
