## What this is

Meta-analysis of binaural-beat experiments (two slightly different pure tones, one per ear). **Full text paywalled** — this card paraphrases the published abstract only (DOI 10.1007/s00426-018-1066-8).

## Abstract-level findings

- 22 studies, 35 effect sizes
- Overall medium effect, **g = 0.45**
- Outcomes mixed across memory, attention, anxiety, and analgesia
- Moderators (per abstract): white/pink-noise masking not necessary; exposure **before** or **before-and-during** a task outperformed exposure **during** the task; longer exposure associated with larger effects; direction and magnitude depend on frequency, time under exposure, and when exposure occurs

## Honest framing

**Not** a Cochrane clinical-disorder review. **Not** evidence that YouTube “frequency + binaural” tracks treat anxiety disorders or “reprogram” the brain. There is a lab/procedural literature with small-to-medium, **protocol-sensitive** effects on anxiety and pain *perception* in some settings — not a treatment guideline. Strength: **mixed / weak** (abstract-only).
